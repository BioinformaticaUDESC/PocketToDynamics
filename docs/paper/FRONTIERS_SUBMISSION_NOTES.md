# Frontiers in Bioinformatics submission notes

Target collection: **Volume II: Methods, Tools and Algorithms in Evolutionary Bioinformatics**.

The public Research Topic page currently lists **1 February 2027** as its manuscript submission deadline and indicates that submissions are open. The authors reported a full APC waiver and a different internal deadline in correspondence; this file does not independently verify that waiver, its conditions, or any administrative deadline. Confirm those details with the collection editorial office before relying on them.

The manuscript is drafted as **Technology and Code**. Frontiers' author guidance gives this type a 350-word abstract and a 12,000-word maximum, and calls for a project link, operating system, programming language, and reuse/licensing restrictions. It also asks for an online repository with a DOI/URI for innovative software submissions. The draft has a 330-word abstract and 7,281 body words before references (excluding title-page metadata and keywords), with nine figures and six tables (15 combined items). Character counts and counting rules are recorded in `MANUSCRIPT_COUNTS.md`. The manuscript describes Linux, Python 3.11 and the absence of an assigned license or public DOI.

Eligibility is a material uncertainty. The Topic page lists many general article types accepted by the journal, including Methods and Technology and Code, but its specific invitation says it seeks mini reviews, systematic reviews, and full reviews. The current manuscript is a software paper, not a review, so do not assume Technology and Code is eligible for this collection without written confirmation from the editors. If the invitation is restricted to reviews, converting this paper would require a genuine critical synthesis with an explicit review method; relabeling the software paper would not satisfy that requirement.

The Topic is explicitly about evolutionary bioinformatics methods, tools and algorithms. PocketToDynamics is primarily a structure-based screening and molecular-simulation workflow; its sequence-conservation analysis is a post-hoc, single-family mapping and does not introduce a new evolutionary method. The present draft states that scope limitation directly. Obtain an editorial scope and article-type check before treating this as a submission-ready manuscript.

## Before submission

- Publish a versioned repository, select and add a software license, and mint a DOI/archival record. Keep third-party structure/data attribution and their terms clear.
- Confirm the waiver, deadline, and article type with the Research Topic editors or journal office.
- Supply author contributions, funding, competing-interest, and corresponding-author details.
- Perform a clean independent installation and reproduce the documented validation data where feasible. The present manuscript explicitly says that this has not yet been done.
- Review the title page, figures/tables count, references, and declarations against the live Frontiers submission form.
